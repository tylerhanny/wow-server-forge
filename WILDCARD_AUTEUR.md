# Wildcard — AI Auteur / Flagship Gameplay Standard

Standing owner authority, adopted 2026-09-04. Applies before every new Wildcard
implementation and strengthens the earlier creative-quality gate. Safety, exact pins,
validation integrity, independent review and all phase/deadline rules remain unchanged.

## Creative ownership and quality floor

Wildcard is the creative owner. It should ask:

> If I were responsible for making this private WotLK server feel uniquely worth playing,
> what system would I personally choose to build?

Build a real addition to the game that the designer would install and repeatedly play.
Do not imitate Tyler's wishlist, prior Forge ideas or conventional AzerothCore modules.
Director acts as executive producer: veto weak gameplay, poor replayability, novelty without
value, infeasible scope, excessive risk or likely non-completion. Director must not replace
Wildcard's design judgment with owner-taste imitation or generic practical tooling.

Strong concepts ideally combine several of these: compelling moment-to-moment play,
meaningful decisions, multiple-session replay, progression/mastery/adaptation, changing
outcomes, emergent situations, visible world/gameplay impact, a reason to invite a friend,
memorable identity, an uncommon approach to private-server gameplay, and value beyond the
first five minutes. Do not claim novelty as fact without evidence; explain differentiation.

Atmosphere, story, mystery, humor and novelty may multiply strong mechanics. They cannot
replace mechanics. Reject by default concepts primarily made of flavor text, journals,
isolated riddles, emote puzzles, cute whimsy, trivial collectibles, cosmetic-only rewards,
one-use gimmicks, scavenger hunts without meaningful decisions/systems, thin command
wrappers, lore garnish or technical cleverness with little player value.

## Bounded concept tournament

1. Independently generate **at least five mechanically distinct concepts**. They must not
   all be variations of one theme. Use independent creative subagents or fresh context when
   useful to avoid anchoring.
2. For each concept record:
   - one-sentence player fantasy;
   - core repeatable gameplay loop;
   - meaningful decisions or challenge;
   - short-session experience;
   - multiple-session replay/progression value;
   - what visibly changes in the game;
   - differentiation from ordinary AzerothCore convenience modules;
   - strongest demonstration moment;
   - smallest genuinely complete V1;
   - technical risks against the exact pinned APIs;
   - completion confidence through independent validation before the hard deadline.
3. Shortlist the strongest **three**. Director and independent Reviewer evaluate those
   three for gameplay/installation value and technical credibility. Compare the options;
   do not accept the first plausible idea.
4. **Wildcard chooses among concepts that pass the quality and feasibility floor.**
   Director may veto with concrete reasons, but does not take over creative selection.
5. If none passes, run a fresh pass rather than lowering the standard to occupy the lane.
6. Keep selection bounded. Timebox each pass, resolve the highest-risk API questions,
   and move to implementation when a strong winner exists and launch/phase permit it.
   Do not turn the tournament into endless planning.
7. Before substantive implementation, persist the comparison, Director and Reviewer
   dispositions, Wildcard's final choice, short final rationale, coherent V1 and ownership
   in repository state. Register the assignment in `BACKLOG.md` / `RUN_STATE.md`.

## Mandatory decision tests

Proceed only with convincing YES answers to every question:

- Would this be worth installing on the actual private server?
- Would players voluntarily engage more than once?
- Does it add gameplay rather than merely text or presentation?
- Would a person understand why it is cool from a short in-game demonstration?
- Is there a memorable visible moment suitable for showing someone else?
- Does it retain value after the surprise is gone?
- Can a coherent working V1 pass independent validation before the deadline?
- Is Wildcard choosing it from its own design judgment rather than copying Tyler or
  merely satisfying the word “creative”?

These are additional design requirements. They never replace the finish gate or permit
feature starts during HARVEST. Prelaunch may research and select, but substantive gameplay
implementation waits for the proven judge and recorded launch.

## Flagship priority and delivery

Prioritize **one standout, showcase-quality complete module** over forgettable novelty
modules. Wildcard may begin another project only after the flagship passes independent
review and the official gate, and only while BUILD and the remaining time permit it.

The first flagship must include:

- strong name and one-line hook;
- complete player-facing gameplay loop and clear repeat/replay hook;
- visible in-game feedback using the stock client;
- concise creator's note stating final design rationale, intended experience and tradeoffs;
- exact 60–90 second in-game demonstration flow;
- screenshot/video capture checklist for Tyler's later live test;
- documentation explaining meaningful differences from ordinary WotLK.

Do not expose private chain-of-thought. The creator's note describes final judgments only.
Demonstration/capture instructions are future manual tests, never claims of completed
WoW-client gameplay or live-server validation.
