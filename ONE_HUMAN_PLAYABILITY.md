# Forge-wide One-Human Playability Authority

**LOCKED OWNER REQUIREMENT — applies to every harvest candidate and every lane.**

Practical, Wildcard, Director-built and all other projects must provide at least one
complete, worthwhile primary experience for Tyler as the only real human: either truly
solo, or with his normally controllable Playerbots at the exact pinned version.
Optional human multiplayer may enhance this complete experience, never unlock its core.

## Non-negotiable boundary

No harvested project may require another human, a second WoW client or human-operated
account, a live human PvP opponent, a guild/raid/community of real people, simultaneous
human actions, or another person operating any role, menu, objective, vehicle, puzzle,
trigger, opposing side or primary-feature demonstration.

One human must be able to discover/access, start/configure, understand the objective,
perform every custom decision/interaction, experience the primary mechanics, succeed or
fail normally, receive/record the intended outcome, retry/replay, and abort/exit cleanly.
A tutorial, spectator mode, debug command, empty arena or partial demonstration does not
qualify. Removing the interesting mechanic to claim solo support does not qualify.

Challenging play is welcome. Do not turn every project into passive/easy content, remove
optional multiplayer, or sacrifice spectacle and meaningful group scaling.

## Playerbots boundary

Normal controllable bots may tank, heal, attack ordinary hostile targets, follow, stay,
fight with the party and respond to supported pinned commands/strategies. Party- or
raid-sized bot groups are allowed where the pinned implementation supports them.

Never assume bespoke intelligence. Bots must not be required to use custom gossip,
make project decisions, solve puzzles, click custom objects on cue, use special items at
exact moments, occupy exact pressure-plate positions, coordinate simultaneous mechanics,
stop damage at a precise health threshold, execute custom raid choreography or understand
new mechanics without ordinary supported behavior.

The single human captain must be able to operate every custom menu, checkpoint, resource,
activation, extraction and unusual mechanic. Where ordinary bot behavior is insufficient,
provide a properly balanced solo mode, automatic roster scaling, module-owned helpers or
encounter mechanisms, or a human-controlled replacement for the unsupported responsibility.
Otherwise reject/defer. Never modify Playerbots source to meet this requirement.

## Proposal and implementation approval

Every proposal must state, before implementation approval:

1. Exact complete one-human gameplay path.
2. Whether its qualifying path is solo or human-plus-Playerbots.
3. Every human responsibility and custom action.
4. Bot/helper responsibilities, if any.
5. Why ordinary pinned behavior/commands suffice, with applicable source evidence.
6. Difficulty and enemy-count scaling.
7. What happens with no bots present; distinguish supported solo from a bots-required path.
8. How the entire primary loop remains available without another real person/client/account.
9. Bot compatibility assumptions requiring later in-game validation.

Director and independent Reviewer must explicitly assess this path before approval.
“Supports parties” is insufficient. Obvious dependence on unsupported bot cognition is a
design rejection, not an issue to leave for Tyler to discover in live testing.

Reassess Stormwright, Holdfast, Warband Arena, Hunt Rhythm and all future proposals.
Do not automatically select/reject a concept. For Stormwright, every environmental/charge
mechanic must be human-operable while bots handle ordinary combat only. Preserve the
project's core gameplay reason for existing when adapting it.

## Mandatory finish-gate documentation

Every candidate README and final handoff must contain a section titled exactly:

```text
One-Human Playability
```

That section must document supported solo configuration, supported human-plus-bots
configuration, required bot number/types if any, all custom actions reserved for the human,
bot assumptions, scaling, limitations, and exact manual smoke steps using only Tyler and
optional Playerbots. Explicitly confirm no second human, second client or second operated
account is required. Mark unsupported optional configurations honestly; at least one
qualifying path must deliver the complete primary loop.

Smoke steps must cover access/start/configure, objectives and custom actions, ordinary
success/failure, progression/score/reward/outcome, retry/replay and clean abort/exit.

Independent Reviewer records **ONE_HUMAN_REVIEW=PASS / FAIL / PENDING**, with the
evaluated path, evidence, limitations and bot assumptions. READY FOR LIVE TEST requires
PASS for design/source/disposable evidence and the rest of the mandatory finish gate.
This disposition must not be supplied solely by the builder.

CI and static review cannot prove actual Playerbots competence or gameplay feel.
Where applicable retain **PENDING LIVE/IN-GAME VALIDATION** for those remaining questions.
Do not fabricate gameplay testing or use that limitation to admit a design already known
to need unsupported cognition.
